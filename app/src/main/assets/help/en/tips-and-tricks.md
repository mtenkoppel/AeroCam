# Tips and tricks

## Prepare before riding

Before you leave:

1. Check the camera's video resolution and field of view.
2. Connect the camera to the Karoo.
3. Lock the camera screen to turn off the displays.
4. Mount the camera securely.

## Avoid fully powering off the camera

Keep the camera connected with its displays locked if you expect to use it again soon.

Use **Put camera to sleep** only when you are finished. AeroCam cannot remotely wake the camera after it fully powers off.

## Give commands time to finish

AeroCam shows a spinner while it waits for the camera. Do not repeatedly press the same control during this state.

Commands can take a moment, especially just after the camera reconnects.

## Turn off camera sounds

Keep awake uses regular QS button presses. Turn off sounds on the DJI camera if you want a calmer ride without hearing the ticks.
