# Tips for getting ready for a ride

1. Have your Karoo on and turn on your DJI camera.
2. AeroCam will automatically connect to your DJI cam. The data field indicates a successful connection.
3. Now lock the screen of the DJI cam. With the right settings, the display turns off and the camera remains available as long as **Prevent standby** is turned on.

![Swipe down on the camera, then tap the highlighted lock.](dji-lock-menu)
