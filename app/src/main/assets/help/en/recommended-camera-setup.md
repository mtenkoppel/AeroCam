# Recommended setup for preventing standby

1. Set **Auto Power Off** to **5 minutes**.
2. Turn off camera sounds (optional): **Settings → Sounds → Mute**.
3. Set **Single Screen Preview** to **Screen off when locked** (the menu may show this as **On**).
4. Connect the camera, then lock its screen. In AeroCam, turn on **Keep camera awake** under **Settings → Prevent standby**.

Menu names may differ slightly between camera firmware versions.
