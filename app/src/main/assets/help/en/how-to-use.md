# How to use AeroCam

## What AeroCam does

AeroCam lets you control a paired DJI Osmo Action camera from your Karoo during a ride.

You can take photos, record video, add highlights and keep the camera ready between recordings. AeroCam also sends live Karoo GPS data to the camera for supported video telemetry.

## Starting a ride

Once the camera has been paired, normal use is simple:

1. Start your ride on the Karoo.
2. Turn on the camera.
3. AeroCam automatically looks for the paired camera and reconnects.
4. Start using the AeroCam data fields when they become active.

If the camera does not reconnect, press **Connect** in the Cam Control data field.

> **Important:** Auto-reconnect can reconnect to a camera that is on. It cannot turn on a fully powered-off camera.

## Choosing a data field

### Cam Control

Cam Control works best as a **full-screen data field**. It keeps the main camera actions, connection status, battery level and card space together in one place.

Use it when camera control is an important part of your ride page.

### Video

Video is designed for a **small data field**. Use it when you mainly want to start or stop recording and add highlights.

It works well on a map page or next to media controls.

### Photo

Photo is also designed for a **small data field**. It gives you a large, simple photo control without taking over the ride page.

It works well on a map page or next to media controls.

The camera cannot take a photo while it is recording video, so Photo is disabled during a recording.

## GPS telemetry

While the camera is connected, AeroCam sends live GPS telemetry from the Karoo. The camera can store supported data with the video for use in DJI telemetry overlays and compatible editing tools.

The available overlays depend on the camera, its firmware and the software used to edit the video.
