# Prevent standby

Your camera turns off when its standby timer runs out—typically after 5 minutes—even while connected.

1. Set auto power off to 5 minutes

Settings → Auto Power Off → 5 minutes

I want a different standby time: Choose the same Auto Power Off time here and on your DJI camera.

2. Turn off sounds (optional)

Settings → Sounds → Mute

3. Enable turning off the display when locked

Settings → Single Screen Preview (Scrn Off when Locked) → On

Swipe down, then tap the lock: bottom row, second icon from the left.

The five-minute camera setting uses a four-minute-thirty-second interval. Choose the matching camera timer in the setup guide for one or two minutes. The camera timer is confirmed by you; AeroCam cannot read it. Prevent standby pauses during recording. Reconnection starts a fresh interval; enabling it while connected or stopping a recording can send an immediate pulse. It cannot wake a powered-off camera.

Keeping the camera awake uses battery. Our tests with a 1,770 mAh battery gave about 4 hours of standby. Actual battery life varies.
